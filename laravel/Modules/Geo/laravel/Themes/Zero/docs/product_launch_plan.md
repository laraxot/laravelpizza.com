# Product Launch Plan - Theme Zero

## Ultra-Minimalist Theme

**Document Version:** 1.0  
**Launch Date:** October 15, 2026 (Target - v1.0)  
**Launch Type:** Major Release (Pre-Alpha to v1.0)  
**Owner:** Theme Product Team

---

## Executive Summary

This document outlines the launch plan for Theme Zero v1.0, marking the transition from pre-alpha to production-ready status. The launch positions Theme Zero as the ultra-minimalist choice for Laravel applications, emphasizing performance, philosophy, and developer control.

### Launch Objectives

1. **Awareness:** Achieve 40% awareness among performance-focused developers
2. **Adoption:** Secure 25 new projects within 90 days
3. **Engagement:** Grow community to 100+ minimalism advocates
4. **Validation:** Obtain 3+ case studies demonstrating performance benefits

## Risk Management

### Launch Risks

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| Critical bug discovered | Low | High | Rollback plan |
| Low initial adoption | Medium | Medium | Niche promotion |
| Philosophy criticism | Low | Medium | Transparent response |
| Infrastructure issues | Low | High | Redundancy |

### Contingency Plans

#### Critical Bug at Launch
1. Acknowledge within 1 hour
2. Deploy rollback if critical
3. Communicate fix timeline
4. Deploy hotfix within 24h
5. Follow-up communication

#### Low Webinar Registration
1. Activate advocate network
2. Increase social frequency
3. Ensure recording available
