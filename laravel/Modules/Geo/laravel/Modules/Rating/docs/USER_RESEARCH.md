## Research Goals

1. Understand rating behavior
2. Identify trust factors
3. Validate review UX
4. Determine incentive effectiveness

| ID | Question | Priority |
|----|----------|----------|

| RQ2 | What makes reviews trustworthy? | P0 |

---

## Methodology

- **Surveys:** 300+ users on rating habits
- **Interviews:** 15 reviewers/non-reviewers
- **Usability Testing:** Rating flow testing
- **A/B Testing:** Incentive variations

---

## Participant Profiles

### Active Reviewer
- **Characteristics:** Leaves reviews regularly
- **Motivation:** Help others, share experience
- **Needs:** Easy process, recognition

### Occasional Reviewer
- **Characteristics:** Reviews for extreme experiences
- **Motivation:** Warn or praise
- **Needs:** Quick process

### Non-Reviewer
- **Characteristics:** Never leaves reviews
- **Barriers:** Time, complexity, no incentive
- **Needs:** Simplicity, motivation

---

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Simplicity Drives Participation
Easy rating process increases reviews.

### Finding 2: Trust Signals Important
Verified badges increase review credibility.

### Finding 3: Timing Matters
Requesting reviews at right moment increases rate.

### Finding 4: Recognition Valued
Reviewers appreciate acknowledgment.

---

## Recommendations

### Immediate
- Simplify rating flow
- Add verification badges
- Optimize request timing

### Long-Term
- Build reviewer recognition program
- Create helpful voting system
- Develop review highlights
