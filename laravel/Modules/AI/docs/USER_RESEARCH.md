# AI Module - User Research

**Module:** AI  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Owner:** Product Team

---

## Research Goals

1. Understand user comfort with AI-generated content
2. Identify high-value AI use cases
3. Validate AI feature UX
4. Determine trust factors

---

## Research Questions

| ID | Question | Priority |
|----|----------|----------|
| RQ1 | Do users trust AI-generated content? | P0 |
| RQ2 | What tasks would users want AI to automate? | P0 |
| RQ3 | How should AI assistance be presented? | P1 |
| RQ4 | What are privacy concerns? | P1 |

---

## Methodology

### User Interviews
- **Participants:** 10-15 users
- **Duration:** 45 minutes
- **Topics:** AI comfort, use cases, concerns

### A/B Testing
- **Tests:** AI vs human content quality
- **Metrics:** Engagement, trust, satisfaction

### Surveys
- **Target:** 200+ users
- **Topics:** AI acceptance, feature priorities

---

## Participant Profiles

### Early Adopter
- **Characteristics:** Tech-savvy, open to AI
- **Needs:** Cutting-edge features
- **Concerns:** Quality, reliability

### Skeptic
- **Characteristics:** Prefers human-created content
- **Needs:** Transparency, control
- **Concerns:** Accuracy, authenticity

### Pragmatist
- **Characteristics:** Open if it works well
- **Needs:** Clear value proposition
- **Concerns:** Cost, privacy

---

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Transparency Matters
Users want to know when content is AI-generated.

### Finding 2: Quality Expectations High
AI content must match or exceed human quality.

### Finding 3: Control Desired
Users want ability to edit/regenerate AI content.

### Finding 4: Privacy Concerns
Users concerned about data used for AI training.

---

## Recommendations

### Immediate
- Label AI-generated content clearly
- Provide edit/regenerate options
- Document AI data usage policies

### Short-Term
- Implement quality feedback loops
- Add human review for critical content
- Create AI preference settings

### Long-Term
- Develop user-specific AI models
- Enable opt-in training data contribution
- Build AI transparency dashboard
