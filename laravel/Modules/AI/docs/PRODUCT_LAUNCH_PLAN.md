# AI Module - Product Launch Plan

**Module:** AI  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Owner:** Product Team

---

## Launch Objectives

1. **Product:** Deploy AI content generation for markets
2. **User:** Improve content quality and consistency
3. **Business:** Reduce manual content creation costs by 50%
4. **Technical:** Maintain <1s latency for AI operations
