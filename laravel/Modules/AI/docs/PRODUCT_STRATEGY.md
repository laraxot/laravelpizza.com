# AI Module - Product Strategy

**Module:** AI  
**Version:** 1.0.0  
**Owner:** Product Team

---

## Executive Summary

The AI module provides intelligent capabilities across the platform, from content generation to fraud detection. This strategy outlines our approach to building AI features that deliver measurable value while managing costs and risks.

---

## Strategic Pillars

### Pillar 1: Value-First AI
Every AI feature must demonstrate clear ROI.

### Pillar 2: Cost Efficiency
Optimize for cost per useful output.

### Pillar 3: Human-in-the-Loop
AI assists, humans decide for critical operations.

### Pillar 4: Continuous Improvement
Models improve with usage and feedback.

## Risks and Mitigation

| Risk | Probability | Impact | Mitigation |
|------|-------------|--------|------------|
| **Model hallucinations** | Medium | High | Human review, confidence scores |
| **Cost overruns** | Medium | Medium | Rate limits, caching, budgets |
| **Bias in models** | Medium | High | Regular audits, diverse training data |
| **API dependency** | Low | High | Multi-provider strategy, fallbacks |
