## Story

Come **maintainer del panel admin**,
voglio **un XotBaseWidget SuperAdmin con vista e traduzioni**,
così che **lo si possa montare nel user menu senza alias Livewire HTTP**.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. Esiste `Modules\User\Filament\Widgets\Profile\SuperAdminWidget` che estende `XotBaseWidget` (non `Filament\Widgets\Widget`, non `XotBaseSchemaWidget`). [Source: architecture.md#adr-001] [Source: prd.md#FR-001]
2. `protected static bool $isDiscovered = false`. [Source: architecture.md#adr-001] [Source: prd.md#FR-006]
3. `mount()` carica `XotData::make()->getProfileModel()` e `url()->current()`. [Source: tech-spec.md#superadminwidget]
4. `toggleSuperAdmin()` chiama `$this->profile->toggleSuperAdmin()` e `redirect($this->url, 303)`. Nessuna Action nuova. [Source: architecture.md#adr-002] [Source: prd.md#FR-002]
5. Vista in `resources/views/filament/widgets/profile/super-admin.blade.php` (namespace `user::filament.widgets.profile.super-admin`): stessi due `icon-button` di oggi, tooltip da lang `user::super_admin_widget.tooltip.active` / `.negated`. [Source: ux-design.md] [Source: prd.md#FR-005]
6. `AdminPanelProvider.php` **non** è modificato in questa story. [Source: tech-spec.md#story-list]
